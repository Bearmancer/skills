# Popular (rock, pop, jazz, electronic): source order

Research and verification only; no recommend mode.

1. Releases, sessions, credits, catalog numbers: skill `music-lookup-artists` `references/routing.md` (method: `../rules.md` "Discography").
2. Label sites + liner notes (scans, label catalog pages): credits, dates, tie-breaks.
3. Artist primaries: official site, interviews in named publications.
4. Press of record: contemporaneous reviews and features (Rolling Stone, Pitchfork, DownBeat, The Wire, NME).
5. Wikis last, paired with second source.
